# Kimbo — 3-minute demo script

The story: **report → food focus → log a meal → Kimbo notices → progress moves.**
Use the sample report and the thali photo in `demo-assets/` so every step is predictable.

## 0. Launch (10 s)
- Cold-start the app: Kimbo hops, the name writes in, the app opens.
- Say: *"Kimbo is a food habit app for Indian meals. One idea: your blood report decides what to eat more of, and every meal shows whether it helped."*

## 1. Start screen (10 s)
- Point at the thali: dishes get labelled one by one.
- Say: *"Logging a thali is usually ten searches. Here it's one photo — and nothing saves until you check it."*
- Tap **Get started**.

## 2. Onboarding (40 s)
- Goal: **Lose weight** (cards advance on tap — no Next buttons).
- Sex, age, height (toggle **ft / in ↔ cm** once), weight on the rulers. *"No keyboard, so no invalid input."*
- Activity: point at the kcal on each option. *"Each answer shows what it does to your day."*
- Goal weight, then pace: *"Each pace shows the date you'd get there; paces that would go under 1,200 kcal say so."*
- Reveal: target in a ring split by protein/carbs/fat. Open **Why this number?** for the formula.
- **Looks good.**

## 3. Blood report (30 s)
- The optional report step appears. Tap **Try a sample report** (or upload `demo-assets/report.pdf`).
- Review screen: *"Every value is shown for confirmation — Kimbo never acts on a misread number."*
- **Use these values** → focus reveal: **More fibre-rich meals**, with the reason.
- *"Kimbo doesn't diagnose. It turns three markers into one thing to do at lunch."*

## 4. Log a meal (40 s)
- Tap the green **+**. Choose **Gallery** → the thali photo.
- Review: tap **Roti**, change 4 → 3 in the sheet; remove the pickle.
- *"AI identifies; deterministic code prices it from an Indian food list, so the same meal is always the same number."*
- **Save lunch** → toasts: *"Fixed. Saved as you ate it."* and *"That helped today's fibre focus ↑"*.
- Today: the focus card shows **1 of 1 meals helped**, and Kimbo grows a leaf.

## 5. Progress (25 s)
- Tap **Log weight** on the goal card; nudge the ruler; save → the goal bar moves.
- Progress tab: week dots, forgiving streak (*"one missed day doesn't break it"*), focus %, weight trend, badges with how to earn them.

## 6. The sample week (15 s)
- You → **Try sample data** → confirm. A full week appears instantly: streak, milestones, focus history.
- *"This is the fastest way for a reviewer to see the whole loop."*

## 7. Charm (5 s)
- On Today, tap Kimbo three times: **"I track food, not personal boundaries. 😭"**

## If something goes wrong
- No network / AI down: every AI step has a retry and a manual path (type it, food list, type report values).
- Reset the app: You → Try sample data / Use my own data.
